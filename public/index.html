Welcome to EduCrate

EduCrate is a platform designed exclusively for the 2024 Batch CS2 students of College of Engineering Kallooppara.

Why EduCrate?

Finding study materials in multiple WhatsApp groups can be frustrating. Notes are often scattered across different chats, making them difficult to locate when you need them. On top of that, files are shared in various formats, which may not always be easy to open on every device.

EduCrate solves these problems by bringing all your study materials together in one place.

All notes are organized in a single platform.

Every document is standardized to PDF format for maximum compatibility.

PDFs can be viewed easily on both mobile phones and computers.

Download your notes anytime for offline access.

No more searching through countless WhatsApp messages to find a single file.

With EduCrate, your study materials are always organized, accessible, and ready whenever you need them.
